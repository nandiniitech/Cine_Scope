# 🎬 Netflix Content Data Analysis

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the Netflix titles dataset — looking at what kind of content is on the platform, how the catalog has grown over time, and where it comes from.

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset to answer a set of business-style questions:

- How many Movies vs TV Shows are on Netflix?
- How has the content library grown over the years?
- What are the most common content ratings?
- Which countries and genres contribute the most titles?
- Who are the most frequent directors?
- What's the typical movie length?
- How has Netflix's content-adding pace changed over time?

## 🗂️ Dataset

`netflix_titles.csv` (included in this repo) — contains title, type (Movie/TV Show), director, cast, country, date added, release year, rating, duration, and genre categories. Originally sourced from the [Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset on Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows).

## 📊 Analyses Covered

All charts are saved automatically to the `images/` folder when the notebook runs.

## ✅ Key Findings

- Netflix has more Movies than TV Shows in its content library.
- The number of Netflix titles increased significantly after 2015, showing rapid platform growth.
- TV-MA is the most common content rating, indicating a large focus on mature audiences.
- The United States contributes the highest number of titles on Netflix.
- Drama is among the most common genres available on the platform.
- Most Netflix movies have a duration between 80 and 120 minutes.
- Netflix added a large number of titles between 2016 and 2020, showing aggressive content expansion during this period.
